The opportunity

The best model on the internet, gated by cost.

Vertical marketplaces — Etsy for X, Airbnb for Y, Amazon for Z — generate winner-take-all network effects, recurring transaction revenue, and high gross margins. But the typical build cost — $250K to $1M and 6–12 months — kills most attempts before launch.

Why now, specifically

Three shifts changed the
math in 2026.

Generation, not hand-coding

Generative AI can scaffold entire applications from natural-language prompts.

Infrastructure as configuration

Modern infrastructure — Supabase, Stripe Connect, Next.js, Vercel — turns industrial-grade backends into a configuration concern, not a build concern.

Agents that run operations

Open-source AI agents now run moderation, fraud detection, listing intelligence, and growth optimization that once required dedicated teams.

ZenVend is the platform that operationalizes all three.

Why now

Three tailwinds, all pointing
the same way.

Cost compression

AI-native scaffolding collapses a six-figure, six-month build into days — putting a real marketplace within reach of a single operator.

Distribution shift

Vertical communities — Substack, Discord, podcasts — have devoted audiences but no platform to monetize them. The marketplace is the missing layer.

Trust premium

Buyers increasingly prefer curated niche marketplaces over sprawling generalists — and pay for the trust that curation brings.

Who's behind it

Built by operators,
for operators.

ZenVend is built by founder Michael Leto and team. It grew out of a simple observation inside a working dev firm: a large share of the build requests coming through were people trying to spin up a marketplace — and every one of them hit the same two-sided walls. So instead of a generic app builder, we built one that deeply understands supply and demand, payouts, trust, and moderation out of the box.
