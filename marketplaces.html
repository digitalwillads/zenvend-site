Who it's for

Four operators,
one platform.

Vertical marketplaces — Etsy for X, Airbnb for Y — are among the most profitable models on the internet. ZenVend makes launching one a matter of days.

01

Vertical marketplace operator

A founder spots an underserved vertical — say sustainable kids' clothing — and launches in days instead of quarters. ZenVend takes a 5–15% commission on each transaction; the founder focuses entirely on demand generation.

02

Agency or studio

A digital agency packages marketplace-as-a-service for its clients. Each client gets their own marketplace on the agency's deployment, priced as a monthly subscription plus a usage fee.

03

Enterprise white-label

A retailer, association, or media company launches a branded marketplace for its existing audience — a private vendor network, B2B procurement portal, or internal exchange — without stitching together five vendors.

04

Community monetization

A newsletter, podcast, Discord, or association launches a vetted marketplace exclusively for members. Vendors pay an application fee for curated access; the operator keeps the commission.
