The whole picture

Capabilities at
a glance.

Everything a real marketplace needs — generation, vendors, buyers, money, safety, and operations — in one calm system.

Marketplace generation

  • AI-prompt to branded marketplace in 60–90 seconds
  • 14+ design styles, fully themeable (colors, typography, layout)
  • Curated initial catalog with verified product photography
  • Custom-domain support and SEO-friendly two-pass HTML

Vendor & seller flow

  • Self-service vendor application with admin approval queue
  • Per-vendor Stripe Connect with destination charges & fees
  • Vendor portal: listings, orders, payouts, settings
  • Configurable platform commission per marketplace
  • Vendor suspension, dispute routing, audit trail

Buyer experience

  • Search, browse, filter, save, message
  • Stripe Checkout with full payment-method support
  • Order tracking, returns, disputes
  • Per-marketplace branding on every buyer page

Payments & money movement

  • Stripe Connect Express for vendor KYC & payouts
  • Destination charges with application fees
  • AES-256-GCM encryption for vendor keys in "own keys" mode
  • Webhook handlers for every relevant Stripe event
  • Stripe Billing for the operator's own subscription

Security & compliance

  • Postgres row-level security on every table
  • Server-side auth with httpOnly cookies (Supabase SSR)
  • Per-tenant data isolation at the database layer
  • Stripe-hosted KYC keeps PCI scope off the platform
  • Audit logging on all admin actions

Operations

  • Admin console: listings, users, orders, payments, vendors, agents
  • Daily & weekly automated reports
  • Transactional email (Resend with custom-domain verification)
  • Cron-driven workflows on Docker or Vercel
  • Dashboards for orders, GMV, fraud, payouts
